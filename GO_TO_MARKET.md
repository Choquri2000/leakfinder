## Landing copy
**Headline:** Find the money your bank statement is hiding.
**Sub:** Drop in a CSV from any bank. In seconds see every subscription, the ones that quietly raised their price,
charges that look doubled, and fees you paid. No login. Nothing stored. Free.
**Bullets:**
- Your yearly subscription bill in one number
- A "review these first" list ranked by money at stake
- Runs in your browser session; your file is never saved or sent to an AI
**Footer:** Information tool, not financial advice.

## Who it helps
- People who suspect they pay for things they forgot (streaming, apps, gyms, cloud storage)
- Anyone after a price-rise season who wants to know what went up
- Freelancers and small households doing a yearly money tidy-up
- Anyone wary of connecting a bank account to an aggregator app

## Honest validation plan (before investing more)
1. Run it on 5-10 friends' real statements (with consent, on their screen) - did it find something they did not know?
2. Record: files that failed to parse, wrong merchants, false positives. Fix the top 3 failure formats.
3. Post for feedback in communities where the rules allow it (e.g. personal-finance or frugal subreddits, indie hacker forums) - one genuine post, ask what bank formats broke.
4. Success signals: 20+ unprompted uses, at least 5 people report a charge they cancelled. If not, revisit the idea.
Nothing here is guaranteed; savings depend entirely on the user's own statement.
